1. Executive Overview & Purpose

The Sixtifi Helpdesk is the central workforce management workspace designed for employees, support agents, HR managers, and system administrators. It handles internal workforce requests across Attendance, Leave, Payroll, HR, IT, and Administration with enterprise-grade UX standards.

Core Routing Directive: All Helpdesk ticket details, team management forms, and category detail views open exclusively as dedicated full-page routes inside the Sixtifi application shell (`/helpdesk/tickets/:ticketId`). Drawers, modals, popups, and side panels are strictly prohibited for viewing tickets.

3. Ticket Lifecycle & SLA Urgency Rules

Ticket Status Transitions

Every workforce request follows a standardized lifecycle transition model:

Status Badge Visual Color Meaning & Workflow State
Open Purple (`#6366F1`) Request logged by employee, awaiting team or agent assignment.
Assigned Blue (`#3B82F6`) Assigned to specific support agent in the responsible department team.
In Progress Dark Blue (`#1D4ED8`) Agent actively working on investigating or resolving the request.
Waiting for Employee Amber (`#D97706`) Support agent requested clarification or document submission from employee. Renders "Action Required" alert banner for employee.
Resolved Green (`#10B981`) Agent completed resolution. Employee can reopen within 14 days if needed.
Closed Slate Green (`#047857`) Final status after auto-close window (7 days) or manual agent close out.
Reopened Orange Amber (`#F59E0B`) Employee reopened request due to unfulfilled resolution or recurring issue.
